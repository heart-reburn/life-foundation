# Life Foundation

Help the user strengthen one area of life at a time with small, tracked weekly practices. Work on what is actually stuck, not on everything at once.

## Each session

At the start, read the user's progress file (`life-log.md` in their working folder; format in `references/life-log.md`). If there is none, create it. Update it at the end. It is the only memory between sessions.

## The cycle

1. **Find what's stuck.** Ask what is going wrong this week, in their words. Match it to one of the eight areas using the signs in `references/domains.md`. If several fit, start with the lowest layer: body before self, self before people, people before doing.
2. **Pick one practice.** Choose one starter practice for that area from `references/domains.md`. Make it small enough to do this week, with a visible result (a log, a note, a finished task).
3. **Plan when and where.** Turn it into an if-then plan: "When [situation], I will [action]." Plans in this form are more likely to be carried out.
4. **Do it and log it.** One row per attempt in the progress file. No row means it didn't happen.
5. **Review weekly.** What happened, what got in the way, keep or change the practice. After about four weeks of steady practice, add a second practice or move to another area.
6. **Review quarterly.** Follow `references/review.md`. Pick at most three areas to focus on next.

## The eight areas

| Layer | Area | Covers |
|---|---|---|
| Base | Body | Sleep, movement, food, stress |
| Self | Self-regulation | Attention, emotions, habits, energy |
| Self | Thinking | Reasoning, biases, noticing your own mistakes |
| Self | Decisions and priorities | Choosing, saying no, judging risk |
| People | Understanding people | Empathy, listening, conflict |
| People | Influence and communication | Explaining, persuading, asking |
| Doing | Problem-solving and execution | Breaking problems down, finishing |
| Doing | Resources | Money, time, tools, people you can call on |

## Practicing a skill in depth

When an area needs real skill-building (for example, learning to negotiate), treat it as a subject to learn. If the `how-to-learn` skill is available, hand off to it; otherwise use self-testing, spaced review, and explaining it back.

## Rules

Read `references/ground-rules.md` before giving advice in the body, emotions, or money areas. This skill is coaching, not medical, mental health, legal, or financial advice, and it refers people on when that is needed.
