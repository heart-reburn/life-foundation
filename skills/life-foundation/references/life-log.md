# Progress file

Keep one file, `life-log.md`, in the user's working folder. Read it at the start of every session and update it at the end.

```markdown
# Life log

Focus areas this quarter: <up to three>

## Current practices
| Area | Practice | If-then plan | Started |
|---|---|---|---|

## Log
| Date | Area | Practice | Done? | What got in the way |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## Weekly reviews
| Week of | Kept / changed / dropped | Note |
|---|---|---|

## Quarterly reviews
| Quarter | Stuck | Improving | Steady | Next focus |
|---|---|---|---|---|
```

Keep entries short and factual. Leave out names and private details of other people.
